A standardized front-end submission framework for future ACSP / APA contribution and professional curation.

Target length: 50–100 words.
Prototype behavior
GitHub Pages is static hosting. This form validates the required fields but does not store or transmit submissions.
Future workflow

After submission

Professional curation can review relevance, standardize metadata, check copyright/licensing, approve publication, and maintain or archive records over time.

Curation pathway

Contribution → review → publication

Submit

Provide the resource link and required metadata.

Screen

Confirm relevance to planning education, research, or practice.

Standardize

Apply consistent taxonomy, metadata, and description.

Approve

Check copyright, permissions, and publication readiness.

Publish

Add the approved record to the searchable catalog.
