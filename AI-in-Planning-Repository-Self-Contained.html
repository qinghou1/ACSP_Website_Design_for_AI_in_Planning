Curation pathway

About

A shared AI knowledge base for planning

The repository is conceived as a professionally curated platform for accessing and contributing AI-related resources across planning education, research, and practice.

Purpose

Why this repository

To accelerate diffusion of planning AI knowledge and infrastructure, especially for smaller academic programs, public-sector planning offices, graduate students, and the wider planning community.

Discoverable

Browse by Education, Research, and Practice and search through a consistent planning-AI taxonomy.

Curated

Resources are intended to be reviewed for relevance, metadata quality, rights, and publication readiness.

Reusable

Links, context, date/version, and permissions information help audiences assess appropriate reuse.
